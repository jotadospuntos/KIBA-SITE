## 2. What is your wording, and what is ours

This matters more than anything else in the review. Two different standards apply.

### Copied word for word from your existing sites — change only if it's actually wrong

- Every **team bio, name, role and tagline**
- On each Capital Solutions page: the **"…in plain terms"** paragraph, every **"This may make
  sense if…"** point, and the **"When this isn't the right tool"** line
- On About Us: the origin line, "what we kept seeing", the vision, **all five values**, "Most
  advisors focus on selling products…", the services list, the "who we serve" list, and the
  closing commitment
- **All three blog articles**, in full
- **All five FAQ answers**
- On Contact: "We're here to help", the invitation line, and the office hours
- **All four testimonials**, and the six "What to expect" cards

If any of the above reads oddly, it probably reads the same way on the current site — worth
checking before you mark it.

### Written new for this site — read these closely

- **Every page headline and the paragraph under it**
- Capital Solutions: the explanatory paragraph after the summary, the **"What it typically funds"**
  lists, the short titles on the sliding cards, the one-line descriptions in the dropdown menu, and
  the three numbered "how we decide" boxes
- About Us: the section headings and the sentences joining your statements together
- The blog index page's headline and framing
- Contact: **"Three ways to start a conversation"** and **"A real person answers. No call centre,
  no ticket queue."**
- The bullet points in the dark hero areas
- The headings on the call-to-action bands at the bottom of each page

## 3. Six things that need your decision

These are flagged deliberately. We did not want to guess.

1. **"Over 50 years of combined lending experience"** (on Meet the Team). We added this up from
   the bios — 26 + 10 + 15. Confirm it's accurate and that you're happy stating it.
2. **"500+ Deals Funded"** appears on the Home page and the Business Acquisitions page. It is not
   on kibadvisors.com anywhere. Is it correct and approved?
3. **"24–72 hours"** for guidance appears on several pages and on your current site. Still true?
4. **"A real person answers. No call centre, no ticket queue."** on Contact — we wrote it. True?
5. **The SMS opt-out keyword in the Privacy Policy** says *"replying OUT to any message"*. We
   reproduced it exactly as supplied, but the industry-standard keyword carriers expect is **STOP**
   — worth confirming with whoever wrote it that OUT is intentional and that STOP is also honoured.
6. **Barbara's title.** The site says *Director of Client Success*, but her bio says *Client
   Success Manager*. Both are on your current site; we kept both rather than picking. Which is
   right?

## 4. Known and intentional — please don't report these

- **Some photos in the large hero areas are still placeholders** — on SBA Loans, Business
  Acquisition Loans, Term Loans, the blog and About Us. Real photography is still being chosen.
  Some look slightly soft; that's the placeholder, not the design.
- **Equipment Financing and Commercial Real Estate show several images in one frame.** That's
  intentional — those two programs cover very different assets, and a single photo would suggest
  we only fund that one thing.
- **There are only four testimonials**, so they repeat as the columns scroll. We deliberately did
  not invent more.
- **Client initials appear in circles instead of photos** — we don't have client photos, and using
  stock faces next to real named quotes would be dishonest.
- **No dollar amounts, rates, terms or qualification thresholds appear anywhere.** Your current
  site doesn't quote any, and we did not invent them. If you want them, send the real numbers.
- **Terms & Conditions still links to kibadvisors.com** — we don't have its text yet. The Privacy
  Policy is now a page on this site, reproduced word for word from the copy you supplied.
- Pages don't appear in Google. That's the `noindex` setting, on purpose for now.

## 5. How to send feedback

The most useful format is one line per item:

> **/capital-solutions/sba-loans** — third card, "Ready for the paperwork" → change to "Comfortable
> with documentation"

Page URL, where on the page, and what it should say. Screenshots with arrows are just as good.

Two things worth calling out if you spot them, because they're easy to miss:
- A **phone number or email** that's wrong anywhere (should be 251-210-8445 /
  info@kibadvisors.com)
- A **button that goes somewhere unexpected**, especially the "Book a Consultation" buttons
- The **Client Portal** button (top right, and in the phone menu) — it opens
  portal.kibadvisors.com/client in a new tab. Worth one click to confirm that's the right
  destination.
